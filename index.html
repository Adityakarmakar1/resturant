Our Story

Where tradition
meets audacity

Born in the hills of Liguria, AURŌ is a love letter to Italian culinary heritage — reimagined through a contemporary lens. Every plate is a conversation between past and future, between earth and sky.

We source exclusively from within 80 kilometers. Our vegetables arrive at dawn. Our fish by mid-morning. Nothing is forced; everything is seasonal, intentional, alive.

Marco
Ferragamo

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

"Food is memory made edible. I cook what my grandmother taught me to feel — not just to know."

Trained under Gualtiero Marchesi and Alain Ducasse, Marco spent twelve years working across three continents before returning to Italy with a singular mission: to distil everything he had learned into the most honest cooking possible.
